Privacy Policy

Put Phone Away respects your privacy. This application is designed to function entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting personal data.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect, store, or share personal data with the developer or any third parties.

The app uses the Usage Access permission to evaluate whether the device is actively used or idle in order to calculate and display phone-free time.

Device activity is determined using local system signals such as screen state, system usage events, audio or video playback, and phone call-related system activity.

  • All data is processed only on your device
  • No data is transmitted externally
  • The developer does not have access to any data

Local Data Storage

All app data (such as recorded idle time and daily records) is stored locally on your device only.

No data is stored on external servers or cloud services.

Permissions

The app uses the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Usage Access – required to determine whether the device is active or idle
  • Foreground Service – required to continuously monitor device state in the background
  • Notifications – used to display a persistent notification indicating that idle-time monitoring is active
  • Boot Completed – used to resume monitoring after device restart

The foreground service operates with an ongoing, visible notification to ensure transparency while monitoring is active.

These permissions are used solely for on-device functionality and are not used for data collection or tracking.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising SDKs.

Data Sharing

No user data is shared with any third parties.

Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

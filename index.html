Privacy Policy

Put Phone Away respects your privacy. This application is designed to function entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting personal data.

Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect, store, or share personal data with the developer or any third parties.

However, the app uses the Usage Access permission to monitor device activity in order to calculate and display time without phone usage (idle time).

  • This data is processed only on your device
  • This data is never transmitted externally
  • The developer does not have access to this data

Local Data Storage

All app data (such as recorded idle time) is stored locally on your device only.

No data is stored on external servers or cloud services.

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Usage Access – to monitor device activity for idle time tracking
  • Foreground Service – to run background monitoring
  • Notifications – to display reminders or updates
  • Boot Completed – to restart monitoring after device reboot

These permissions are used solely to provide the app’s functionality.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising SDKs.

Data Sharing

No user data is shared with any third parties.

Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
